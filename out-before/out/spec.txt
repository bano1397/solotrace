LedgerLite Payments API — Requirements Speciﬁcation
Document ID: LL-SPEC-PAY · Version 2.0 · Status: Approved · Owner: Compliance & Risk Ofﬁce
Supersedes v1.0. Changes in v2.0 follow the internal payments-risk review and are marked CHANGED  or NEW .
1. Purpose
This document lists the functional and compliance requirements for the LedgerLite Payments API. Every
requirement must be implemented in code and covered by at least one automated test before a
production release. Auditors verify this traceability for each release.
2. Scope
Customer accounts, deposits, withdrawals, internal transfers, audit logging, and account statements. All
amounts are in USD.
3. Requirements
ID Requirement Risk
REQ-01 An account can only be created with a unique email address. An
account may not send or receive money until its KYC status is
veriﬁed.
Acceptance: duplicate email is rejected; any money movement
on an unveriﬁed account is rejected.
High
REQ-02 A deposit amount must be greater than 0 and at most 50,000.00
per transaction.
Acceptance: 0, negative and 50,000.01 are rejected; 50,000.00
is accepted.
Medium
REQ-03 A withdrawal must never make the account balance negative.
Acceptance: withdrawing more than the balance is rejected and
the balance is unchanged.
High
REQ-04 A transfer between two accounts is atomic: either both the debit
and the credit happen, or neither does.
Acceptance: if the credit fails, the debit is rolled back.
High
REQ-05 CHANGED A transfer of 5,000.00 or more must be held as pending_approval
until a second, different user approves it. (v1.0 threshold was
10,000.00.)
Acceptance: a 5,000.00 transfer is pending, not completed; the
initiator cannot approve their own transfer.
High
REQ-06 Every money movement (deposit, withdrawal, transfer, approval)
writes an audit log entry containing actor, action, amount,
Highaccount(s) and UTC timestamp. Audit entries cannot be edited.
Acceptance: each operation adds exactly one entry; no update
endpoint exists for audit entries.
REQ-07 An account is locked after 5 consecutive failed PIN attempts. A
locked account cannot move money.
Acceptance: the 5th wrong PIN locks the account; a correct PIN
before that resets the counter.
Medium
REQ-08 All monetary values are stored and calculated as exact decimals
(never ﬂoating point) and returned with exactly 2 decimal places.
Acceptance: 0.10 + 0.20 equals 0.30 exactly; API returns
"0.30".
Medium
REQ-09 NEW Total outgoing transfers per account must not exceed 20,000.00
per UTC calendar day.
Acceptance: a transfer that would take the day's total above
20,000.00 is rejected.
High
REQ-10 NEW Transfers to an account whose country code is on the sanctions
list (KP, IR, SY, CU) are blocked and logged.
Acceptance: the transfer is rejected with reason sanctions and
an audit entry is written.
High
REQ-11 NEW Account statements must mask account numbers, showing only
the last 4 digits (e.g. ****1234).
Acceptance: no full account number appears in any statement
response.
Medium
REQ-12 Audit log entries must never be deleted through the API (7-year
retention).
Acceptance: no delete endpoint exists for audit entries; a
DELETE request returns 405 or 404.
Medium
4. Release rule
A release is approved only when every requirement above is implemented and covered by at least one
passing automated test, with evidence (ﬁle and test name) recorded in the traceability matrix.
